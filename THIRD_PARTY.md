# Third-party assets

None of the files below are stored in this repository.
`elastra-download-assets` downloads them into `assets/` (git-ignored) at pinned revisions and checks every file against the sha256 in `conf/assets/default.yaml`.
Each one stays under its own license.

## Data in this repository derived from third-party work

* `data/initial_states/protomotions.npz`, arrays `train_*`: the 39 ProtoMotions training initial states are lying poses from the HumanUP pose pool (https://github.com/RunpeiDong/HumanUP; the repository is Apache-2.0, the pose files are distributed through a link in it), mapped to the 29-DoF G1 and placed on the ground (see `data/README.md`).
* `data/initial_states/host.npz`: generated by `elastra-make-host-initial-states` with HoST's initial-state randomisation re-implemented in `src/elastra/initial_states.py`.
* `data/load_deflection/vlaovic2024_pur_100_cycles.csv`: digitised from Figure 2 of Z. Vlaović, N. Klarić, D. Domljan, *Applied Sciences* 14, 10016 (2024), https://doi.org/10.3390/app142110016, published under CC BY 4.0 (see `data/README.md`).
